What We're Covering
The Big Ideas

These are the topics we learned about. Some of them are already happening right now, which is kind of crazy.

TOPIC 01

Reusable Rockets

SpaceX figured out how to land rockets after they launch so they can be used again. Before that, rockets were just thrown away after one trip — like a paper plate but way more expensive.

Challenge: Surviving Reentry Heat
TOPIC 02

Rockets and Their Parts

A rocket is more than just a pointy tube. It has a nose cone, fuel tanks, engines, boosters, fins, and a heat shield. Each part has a specific job, and if one fails, the whole mission fails.

Key Part: The Engine
TOPIC 03

Mars Colonization

The idea is to actually build homes on Mars and have people live there permanently. The challenges? Freezing cold, no breathable air, and you're months away from anything familiar.

Travel Time: ~7 Months
TOPIC 04

Space Tourism

Regular people — not just astronauts — are buying tickets to go to space. Private companies like SpaceX and Blue Origin are making it happen. It's very expensive right now, but that's changing.

Companies: SpaceX, Blue Origin
TOPIC 05

Artificial Gravity

In space, there's no gravity, which sounds fun until your bones start disappearing. A spinning space station can create a force that feels like gravity and keeps astronauts healthy on long trips.

Method: Rotation
TOPIC 06

Faster Space Travel

Chemical rockets work, but they're slow. New engines — like ion drives and nuclear rockets — could cut the trip to Mars from 7 months to just 45 days. That's a huge deal.

Tech: Ion & Nuclear Engines
TOPIC 07

Space Mining

Asteroids are packed with rare metals — more gold and platinum than all of Earth's mines combined. Robots could collect those resources and bring them back, which would be worth trillions.

Target: Asteroids
TOPIC 08

AI and Robots in Space

Sending a robot is safer than sending a human. Robots don't need air, food, or sleep. They can explore dangerous places and send back data while humans monitor everything safely from Earth.

Example: Mars Rovers

Did You Know
Wild Space Facts

Things so weird that you'll probably repeat them at lunch. You're welcome.

01
A day on Mars is only 37 minutes longer than a day on Earth. That's the most normal thing about Mars. Everything else is completely different.
02
SpaceX's Starship is taller than the Statue of Liberty. And it's designed to land on Mars and fly back to Earth. Both ways.
03
Asteroid 16 Psyche is estimated to be worth $10 quintillion. That's more than the entire economy of Earth. By a lot. It's mostly made of metal.
04
Astronauts can grow up to 3 inches taller in space because their spine decompresses without gravity. They shrink back when they come home.
05
A nuclear rocket could get to Mars in 45 days instead of 7 months. That's the difference between a summer vacation and most of a school year.
06
If you cry in space, the tears don't fall. They just blob around your eyeball until you wipe them off. Space makes everything awkward.
07
There is zero sound in space. So every explosion you've ever seen in a Star Wars movie is completely made up. Every single one.
08
A SpaceX Falcon 9 rocket has been launched and landed over 20 times on the same rocket. It's like a school bus that also flies to space and comes back for pickup.
09
Sunsets on Mars are blue instead of orange. On Earth, red light scatters. On Mars, it's the opposite — blue light cuts through the dust at sunset.
